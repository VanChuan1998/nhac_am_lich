3. About lunar-date accuracy

The extension computes lunar dates using an astronomical algorithm anchored to UTC+7. The results have been checked against widely used calendar reference points and match. However, different printed calendars sometimes differ by one day in certain months, because of differing rounding conventions or reference time zones.

For occasions that matter — death anniversaries, weddings, family rites — you should check against your family's calendar or whichever source you normally trust. This extension is a reminder tool, not an authoritative source on calendrical rules.
